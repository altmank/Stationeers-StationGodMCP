 Gas hold: once a pipe job ends gas_lost, every later real run that touches a pipe network (a pipe tool, or a place_structure / remove_structure run that places or removes anything with a pipe end) is refused with gas_check_failed until the world is loaded again. acknowledge_gas_lost with the id of the job that set the hold accepts that loss: the hold is lifted as this run starts (a queued run: when it leaves the queue) and the reply's gas_hold repeats the loss (job, networks, missing mol), as does every poll of that job with job_id; an empty string is refused (invalid_argument); another id is refused with gas_hold_mismatch, naming the holding job and its loss. A later failed check holds pipe jobs again, under its own job id. ASK THE USER BEFORE ACKNOWLEDGING A GAS LOSS: never pass acknowledge_gas_lost on your own. A dry run's gas_hold (present when a hold applies to the run or acknowledge_gas_lost is given) says whether the hold applies and what acknowledging would lift; it lifts nothing.
