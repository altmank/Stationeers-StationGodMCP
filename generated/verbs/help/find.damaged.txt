Damaged and broken things in the world, worst first.
Options:
- min_damage (number, default 0): Only damage ratio above this (0.25 yellow, 0.75 red).
- broken_only (boolean, default false): Only things in the game's broken state.
- structures_only (boolean, default false): Leave items out.
- near (string or array): "player" only.
- radius (number): Metres around near.
- limit (integer, default 8): Entries per page.
- offset (integer, default 0): Entries to skip.
