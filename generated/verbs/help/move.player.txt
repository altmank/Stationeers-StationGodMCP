Move a player anywhere at once: to a point, beside a thing or another player.
Reports first; confirm: true does it.
Cheat.
Options:
- player (string): The player to move, by name; default the player.
- at (list): Destination point.
- to (string): Destination: beside this thing (an id).
- to_player (string): Destination: beside this player (a name).
- safe_ground (boolean, default false): Drop the point onto the highest safe surface, 4 m above it.
- confirm (boolean, default false): true acts; false only reports, whatever the call names (typed ids included). Left out, the write rule decides: typed ids act at once where the verb acts on them; a ref, network or spread reports.
Reply: player {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, moved_by, before {position, room_id, room_type, outside}, after {position, room_id, room_type, outside}, destination {form, next_to, safe_ground}, distance_m, note
Example: move {what: "player", to_player: "xceled", confirm: true}
