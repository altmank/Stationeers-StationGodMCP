Take things away as a player would, with refunds and guards. Reports first; confirm: true removes and answers when the job is done.
A report carries report_only: true and lists every target with its current and new value.

whats:
- structure: built structures by id (up to 256, one job), taken apart; every build state is refunded down to the kit
- cable: cable pieces as wire cutters remove them: by id, or the piece in each cell of waypoints or cells
- pipe: pipe pieces as a player remove them: by id, or the piece in each cell of waypoints or cells, never emptying or splitting a network that holds gas
- chute: chute pieces as a player remove them: by id, or the piece in each cell of waypoints or cells
- script: delete a saved script and every version kept beside it

Shaping on every call: fields, omit, list_limits, output_file, whole, repeat (help {topic: "shaping"}).
help {verb: "remove", what} gives a what's options, reply keys and an example.
