Move gas and liquid between atmospheres or rooms, delete it, or make it out of nothing, with no pipe or pump.
Reports first; confirm: true does it.
Cheat.
Options:
- source (string or object): A thing id, "looking_at", "planet" (with delete), "create" (made out of nothing, with to), or a room {room} or {room_of}.
- to (string or object): A thing id, "looking_at" or a room; left out with delete.
- delete (boolean, default false): Destroy the gas instead of moving it.
- gases (list): Gas names as inspect atmosphere gives them; left out, every gas and liquid.
- amount_mol (number): Moles of each gas, capped at what is there; left out, all. Required with source create.
- temperature_k (number): source create: the temperature the made gas arrives at, in kelvin.
- allow (list of burst): burst: move past a burst rating.
- joined (boolean, default true): Treat atmospheres the game mixes every tick as one.
- confirm (boolean, default false): true acts; false only reports, whatever the call names (typed ids included). Left out, the write rule decides: typed ids act at once where the verb acts on them; a ref, network or spread reports.
- max_wait (number, default 30): Seconds to wait for a confirmed job to finish before answering; 0 answers with its job id at once.
Applied at the next atmospherics tick. A confirmed move waits for it (max_wait, default 30 s) and answers status applied or failed; past max_wait it answers queued and its id (inspect job).
source "create" makes amount_mol of each named gas at temperature_k in to, as the console's addgas does, with the same checks on to: move {what: "gas", source: "create", to: "2005300", gases: ["Oxygen"], amount_mol: 500, temperature_k: 293.15, confirm: true}.
Reply: transfer_id, status, predicted, joined, from {members, total, joined_by, room}, to {members, total, joined_by, room}, deleted, moved, error {code, message, data, see}, before, amount_mol, ticks, note
Example: move {what: "gas", source: "2005300", gases: ["Hydrogen"], delete: true}
