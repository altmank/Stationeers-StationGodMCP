A prefab before it stands anywhere: placement kind, turns, cells, ports; its game data and recipes; a list or pattern compares a family.
Options:
- prefab (string or integer or array, required): A whole prefab name or hash; or a list, or parts with * ("*Door*"): a row per loaded prefab, by name, at most 64.
- include (list of small_cells | data | recipes): small_cells: each cell's offset. data: the prefab's own game data, every field the game serializes on it (maxPressureDelta, -1 for no limit; flashpointTemperature, autoignitionTemperature; MaxQuantity; UsedPower; BuildStates with each state's tool and cost) and mass_kg; fields: ["data.maxPressureDelta"] narrows it, output_file takes it whole. recipes: what makes it: each machine, its inputs, time and energy.
One buildable prefab answers its placement view; any other prefab (an item, a kit) its facts; a list or a pattern a row of facts per prefab (prefabs, matched, listed).
recipes inputs are the game's Recipe amounts by name: grams of an ingot (Iron, Copper, Solder), units of a reagent; a furnace recipe adds its temperature_k and pressure_kpa range.
Reply: (a buildable prefab) prefab_name, prefab_hash, display_name, build_states, runtime_type, placement, grid_size_m, small_grid, rotation_axes, allowed_rotations [{facing, up, euler}], small_cell_count, small_cells [{x, y, z}], render_box {min, max, size}, grid_box {min, max, size}, ports [{index, type, role, flow, at, outward}], visual_up {local, lying_allowed, source, verified}, reversible_flow {logic_type, value, reason}, has_cursor, controls {local, votes, considered, source, fallback}, engine {name, max_thrust_n, max_exhaust_velocity_ms, specific_impulse_s, max_fuel_flow_rate, efficiency, efficiency_percent, mass_kg, chamber_volume_l, feed, inputs, max_moles_per_tick, litres_per_tick, max_pressure_per_tick_kpa, flow_rate_min_l, flow_rate_max_l}, data, recipes [{maker, maker_display_name, time_s, energy_j, inputs, temperature_k, pressure_kpa, source}] | (any other prefab) prefab_name, prefab_hash, display_name, runtime_type, buildable, data, recipes [{maker, maker_display_name, time_s, energy_j, inputs, temperature_k, pressure_kpa, source}] | (a list or pattern) prefabs [{prefab_name, prefab_hash, display_name, runtime_type, buildable, data, recipes}], matched, listed
Example: inspect {what: "prefab", prefab: "ItemKitAirlock", include: ["recipes"]}
