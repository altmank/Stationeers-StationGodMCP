Plants in trays, planters and stations: stage, health, problems, time to harvest.
Options:
- id (string or array): One plant, in full.
- include (list of unplanted): unplanted: crops and seeds not planted.
- verbose (boolean, default false): The long form of the reply.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload the one-id reply; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: plants, count, total, offset, has_more, game_time_s, day_length_s, debug_fast_growth
Example: find {what: "plant"}
