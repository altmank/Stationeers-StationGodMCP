List things in the world or an area, filtered, a page at a time, nearest first. Read only.

whats:
- thing: any object: structures, devices, tanks, loose items, players, animals
- item: stackable items and kits with quantity and holders, anywhere: ground, carried, stored, machine stock, silos; a kit such as Kit (Battery Large) is an item to find and move, a built structure is not; group_by sums them
- device: devices with logic, in the world or on one gateway's data networks
- container: outermost holders with at least one item: lockers, crates, machines, a backpack on the floor
- frame: outer frames (a face on outside air), with exposed faces and airtightness
- pad: landing pads for traders and rockets (not power or teleport pads), by the game's checks: size, runway, which ships fit
- water: canisters, tanks, devices and pipe networks holding water or steam, largest first
- ore: deep miner spots: the ore profile at a point, and the nearest spots holding given ores
- food: every food and drink: nutrition, hydration, decay, totals
- plant: plants in trays, planters and stations: stage, health, problems, time to harvest
- room: closed rooms measured cell by cell: volume, pressure, temperature, gases; or the room one thing is in
- gateway: the device scopes: 'world' and each StationGod Gateway with its status
- damaged: damaged and broken things in the world, worst first

Shaping on every call: fields, omit, list_limits, output_file, whole, repeat (help {topic: "shaping"}).
