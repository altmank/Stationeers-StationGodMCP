Price a removal of cable, pipe or chute pieces without doing it, ever: ids, cells or a whole network.
Options:
- kind (cable | pipe | chute, default cable): The pieces' kind.
- id (string or array): Pieces to price.
- waypoints (list): The pieces along these lines.
- cells (list): The piece in each cell.
- network (string or object): Every piece of this network, up to 1024.
- root (string): For would_split's cut-off; default every supplier.
- allow (list of split): split: report would_split as allowed.
- materials (string or array): Holder(s) that pay materials and take source refunds, tried in order; ids or "player". Default the player.
- refund_to (string or integer or array, default [inventory, source, storage, ground]): Where removed materials go: a word (inventory, source, storage, ground, none), a container id, or a list tried in turn.
- include (list of links | notes | network_devices): Parts to add to the reply.
- rows (integer, default 5): Pieces listed; 0 for none.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, ...} or {index, ok, reference_id, error}], count, success_count, error_count}; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: tool, status, job_id, grade, ready, problems [{code, message, reference_id, at}], warnings [{code, message, reference_id, at}], cells_total, placed, changed, kept, removed_count, air_cells, cells [{at, action, prefab_name, rotation_deg, shape, ends, cost, refund, existing, joins, open_end, flow}], removals [{reference_id, prefab_name, at, network_id, refund, assumed}], materials {from, needed, refund_enabled, refund, refund_plan}, networks_before, networks_after [{index, networks_before, new_pieces, device_count, port_count, devices, guard}], would_bridge [{kind, networks, device, ports, allowed}], would_split [{network_id, parts, cut_ports, allowed, components, root, cut_off}], links {count_before, count_after, added, lost, added_count, lost_count, model_matches_game, model_differences}, notes
Example: plan {what: "removal", kind: "cable", network: "3768865"}
