The reply cap: a direct reply over 12 KB to a call that asked for no size is cut to about 4 KB; a report, whole: true, output_file and list_limits are never cut. A cut reply keeps as much as fits, in this order: whole entries of its main list; else every entry in a shallow form (plain values kept, nested lists as their length, small objects kept, others as {...}); else the first shallow entries. With several lists, each is cut in proportion. It adds ref, cut ("8384 B; cells 4 of 8 shallow, pieces 9 of 18 shallow") and next: whole: repeat 'r4' with whole: true (~2620 tokens) | page: repeat 'r4' with offset: 8 | as a target, r4 is the shown entries.
fields [keys]: keep only these keys; a dotted path goes deeper. omit [paths]: leave keys out. list_limits {list: n}: keep more of a list cut by default. output_file: true or a file name: the whole reply to a file, answered by a pointer. id, prefab and label read as reference_id, prefab_name and custom_name (else display_name); a near miss is read as its one close key (fields_mapped says so); other unknown names come back in fields_unmatched with fields_closest. Errors are never cut or shaped.
