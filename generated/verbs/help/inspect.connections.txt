One pipe, cable, chute or device: its ends and what each joins.
Options:
- id (string or array, required): Target: an id, a list of ids, a ref ("r3": the ids that reply showed; "r3.rockets.parts"), or "looking_at" (the thing the crosshair is on); "player" where the what says.
id may be a list or a ref: the reply is {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload one reply; fields name its keys.
Each end: position is the end's own point; at is the centre of the cell a piece joining it stands in (as build reports a port's at) and piece_end the end that piece needs, pointing back into this thing: build pipe {piece: {at, ends: [piece_end, ...]}} joins it.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload the one-id reply; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: thing {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, position {x, y, z}, rotation {facing, up, euler}, own_network {kind, id}, color {index, name, is_default}, ends [{index, type, type_name, role, role_name, position, at, piece_end, network, connected}], count
Example: inspect {what: "connections", id: ["2514519", "3575779"]}
