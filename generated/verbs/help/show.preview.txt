Wire boxes of a planned structure layout (build structure's dry run), plus cells and boxes.
Options:
- placements (list): As build structure's placements.
- prefab (string or integer): Prefab: a whole name, a hash, or parts with * ('*Battery*', '*Tank*Insulated*': each part, any order); or a list of them.
- at (array or object or string): A point, "looking_at", or a relative or look-face form (help positions).
- rotation (list): [x, y, z] degrees, multiples of 90.
- facing (string): Where the front points (as build structure).
- up (string): Where the top points (as build structure).
- face (string): Face-placed pieces: the cell face it sits on.
- orient (object): {mount, upright, controls_toward, ports: [{role, index, type, toward}], flow: {from, to}}: choose the turn by intent. mount: wall, floor, ceiling or an axis; a target (controls_toward, toward, from, to): an axis, room, player, a point or a thing's id.
- above_floor (number): Bottom this many metres above the floor below at.
- build_state (string or integer): finished (default), first, or a state index.
- allow (list of door_keepout): Guards to lift, by name.
- cells (list): Yellow 0.5 m cubes at these points.
- boxes (list): {min, max, color} boxes.
- seconds (number, default 30): How long to show.
- keep (boolean, default false): Keep what earlier calls drew.
- clear (boolean): Only remove what is drawn.
- xray (boolean, default false): Draw everything, boxes and cells too, through walls, frames and terrain.
Reply: shown, cleared, seconds, plan {ready, problems, warnings, placements, materials, from, free, notes}, notes, drawn_on, view {source, age_s}
Example: show {what: "preview", prefab: "StructureWallLight", at: [716, 203, 668], facing: "-x"}
