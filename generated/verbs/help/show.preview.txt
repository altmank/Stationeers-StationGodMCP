A planned structure layout or cable, pipe or chute run (build's dry run) as wire boxes or see-through ghosts, plus cells and boxes.
Options:
- placements (list): As build structure's placements; each may give its own tint, and its label shows as the ghost's tooltip.
- prefab (string or integer): Prefab: a whole name, a hash, or parts with * ('*Battery*', '*Tank*Insulated*': each part, any order); or a list of them.
- at (array or object or string): A point, "looking_at", or a relative or look-face form (help positions).
- rotation (list): [x, y, z] degrees, multiples of 90.
- facing (string): Where the front points (as build structure).
- up (string): Where the top points (as build structure).
- face (string): Face-placed pieces: the cell face it sits on.
- orient (object): {mount, upright, controls_toward, ports: [{role, index, type, toward}], flow: {from, to}}: choose the turn by intent. mount: wall, floor, ceiling or an axis; a target (controls_toward, toward, from, to): an axis, room, player, a point or a thing's id.
- above_floor (number): Bottom this many metres above the floor below at.
- build_state (string or integer): finished (default), first, or a state index.
- label (string): What the placement is, shown as a tooltip when the crosshair is on its ghost (as build structure's label).
- run (object): A cable, pipe or chute run to plan and draw: build cable, pipe or chute's options with what ({what: "pipe", grade, waypoints, ...}); nothing is built.
- from_plan (string): A ref of a plan route or plan spot reply ("r9"; one spot: "r9.spots.2", default the first): preview its build_arguments; a route becomes run, a spot the placement, and options given here replace the plan's.
- style (boxes | ghost | both, default boxes): boxes: footprint, body and port wire boxes; ghost: the structures' own meshes at the build state, see-through and tinted; both.
- tint (string or array): The ghosts' colour, yours to choose (no meaning of its own): a palette name (show highlight's), [r, g, b] or [r, g, b, a] each 0 to 1; alpha 0.4 when left out.
- allow (list of door_keepout): Guards to lift, by name.
- cells (list): Yellow 0.5 m cubes at these points.
- boxes (list): {min, max, color} boxes; color: red, green, white, cyan, yellow, any tint name, or [r, g, b] / [r, g, b, a].
- seconds (number, default 30): How long to show.
- keep (boolean, default false): Keep what earlier calls drew.
- clear (boolean): Only remove what is drawn.
- xray (boolean, default false): Draw everything through walls, frames and terrain: boxes and cells whole, ghosts fainter where hidden. A run's ghosts always get the see-through pass (pieces sit inside floors and frames).
- verbose (boolean, default false): plan: build structure's whole report of each placement (orient alternatives, port checks, layout, cost) in place of the brief one.
Materials are not checked: ready means build would place it. A new call replaces what the last drew (keep: true adds); ghosts counts this call's ghosts, ghosts_total every preview ghost on screen.
Reply: shown, ghosts, ghosts_total, cleared, seconds, plan {ready, problems, warnings, placements}, run {what, ready, pieces, problems, warnings}, notes, drawn_on, view {source, age_s}
Example: show {what: "preview", prefab: "StructureWallLight", at: [716, 203, 668], facing: "-x"}
