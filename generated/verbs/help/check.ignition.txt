Whether carried items, or a tank or network by id, would catch fire in the air around them.
Options:
- id (string or array): A tank, canister or pipe network: its contents' autoignition margin; left out, the player's carried items.
- include (list of prefabs): prefabs: every prefab's ignition temperatures.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload the one-id reply; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: (Carried) player {reference_id, display_name, position}, cell {temperature_k, pressure_kpa, energy_j, inflamed, oxygen_mol, in_room}, autoignition_min_energy_j, minimum_ignition_pressure_kpa, items [{reference_id, prefab_name, display_name, slot, in_hand, holder, atmosphere, burning, hidden, flashpoint_k, flashpoint_effective_k, autoignition_k, ignites_now, health_ratio}], prefabs [{prefab_name, display_name, flashpoint_k, autoignition_k}] | (Contents) reference_id, kind, temperature_k, pressure_kpa, autoignition_k, margin_k, ignites_now | (BatchResult) results [{index, ok, error}], count, success_count, error_count
Example: check {what: "ignition", id: "2005300"}
