Paint things as a spray can does; network: a pipe's id, or a device's with kind, paints that network's pieces; with no target, list the colours.
Options:
- id (string or array): Things to paint the one color, or a ref.
- network (string or object): A pipe, cable or chute network (its id, a piece on it, or a device with kind): paints its pieces.
- kind (pipe | cable | chute): With network given as a device: which of its networks.
- include (list of devices): devices: also paint the network's devices.
- color (string): A colour name, an index, or 'default'.
- items (list): {id, color} pairs, a colour each.
- confirm (boolean, default false): true does it; left out, the call only reports what it would do.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload the one-id reply; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: (PaintColors) colors [{index, name, paint_only}], count | (BatchResult) results [{index, ok, error}], count, success_count, error_count
Example: write {what: "paint", network: "4639870", kind: "pipe", color: "Red"}
