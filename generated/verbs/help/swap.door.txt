Replace doors with another door prefab in place (glass door to airlock) without opening the doorway; keeps Open, Lock and the label, and repoints the boards, pins and logic units that pointed at the old door.
Reports first; confirm: true does it.
Options:
- id (string or array): The doors (up to 4096); or room.
- room (string): Every door in or around this room.
- into (string, required): The new door prefab (StructureAirlock, StructureGlassDoor...): Door, Airlock or UnPoweredDoor.
- prefab (string or integer or array): Only pieces of these prefabs; the others are kept.
- materials (string or array): Holder(s) that pay materials and take source refunds, tried in order; ids or "player". Default the player.
- skip_unmatched (boolean, default false): Leave pieces that cannot change as they are instead of refusing.
- refund_to (string or integer or array, default [inventory, source, storage, ground]): Where removed materials go: a word (inventory, source, storage, ground, none), a container id, or a list tried in turn. Left out with no player and no materials: none.
- include (list of notes): Parts to add to the reply.
- rows (integer, default 5): Pieces listed per list.
- verbose (boolean, default false): The long form of the reply.
- confirm (boolean, default false): true acts; false only reports, whatever the call names (typed ids included). Left out, the write rule decides: typed ids act at once where the verb acts on them; a ref, network or spread reports.
- queue (boolean, default true): Wait in line behind a running job (up to 8) instead of answering busy.
- max_wait (number, default 30): Seconds to wait for a confirmed job to finish before answering; 0 answers with its job id at once.
A door is swapped as a wall is: the new door is built in place and raised to its last state while the game tick is held, and only then is the old door destroyed; a door on faces must bear each face's pressure difference. Docks, manual hatches and window shutters are never touched.
Before the old door goes, the swap hands over what points at it: boards linked to it (airlock and door control: their linked doors, exterior and interior), IC housing pins, and logic readers, writers and mirrors (their device); its label; Open and Lock. Each piece's handover lists them with action repoint or keep (report) and repointed, restored, still_points or failed (job). still_points is what the swap cannot move: a chip whose program names the old id, a batch write by the old prefab hash when the new door is another prefab, any other field that holds the old door; its note says what to change.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload the one-id reply; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: (JobQueued) a JobQueued (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "JobQueued"}) | (StructureSwapReport) target, ready, problems [{code, message, reference_id}], warnings [{code, message, reference_id}], pieces_total, to_swap, kept, unmatched, pieces [{reference_id, prefab_name, position, rotation_deg, build_state, target_prefab_name, target_build_state, blocks_air_before, blocks_air_after, blocks_gravity_before, blocks_gravity_after, air_change, seals, stressed, faces, room_ids, materials, handover}], pieces_listed, by_prefab [{prefab_name, target_prefab_name, count}], kept_pieces [{reference_id, prefab_name, reason, message}], unmatched_pieces [{reference_id, prefab_name, reason, message}], from {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, materials [{prefab_name, display_name, cost, refund, charge, give_back, available, paid_by, stacks}], refund_enabled, refund_plan, rooms [{room_id, cell_count, total_mol, energy_j}], notes | (JobBusy) a JobBusy (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "JobBusy"}) | (SwapJob) a SwapJob (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "SwapJob"}) | (JobDropped) a JobDropped (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "JobDropped"})
