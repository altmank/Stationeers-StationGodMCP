Devices with logic, in the world or on one gateway's data networks.
Options:
- gateway (string, default world): "world" (default) or a gateway id: only devices on its data networks.
- prefab (string or integer or array): Prefab: a whole name, a hash, or parts with * ('*Battery*', '*Tank*Insulated*': each part, any order); or a list of them.
- name (string): Part of the shown name or label, any case.
- limit (integer, default 25): Entries per page.
- offset (integer, default 0): Entries to skip.
Paging and prefab parts are applied by the verb layer to the handler's whole list (it walks every device either way).
Reply: devices [{reference_id, prefab_name, prefab_hash, display_name, runtime_type, slot_count, is_gateway, worn_by, worn_slot}], count, total, offset, limit, has_more, gateway_id
Example: find {what: "device", prefab: "*GasSensor*"}
