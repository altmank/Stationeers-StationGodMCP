Damage state of things, or of every piece of a network, worst first.
Options:
- id (string or array): Up to 256 things.
- network (string or object): A network: its id, a piece or device on it, or {id, port}.
- kind (pipe | cable | chute, default pipe): The network's kind.
- damaged_only (boolean, default false): Network: only damaged and broken pieces.
- limit (integer, default 8): Entries per page.
- offset (integer, default 0): Entries to skip.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload the one-id reply; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: (Health) reference_id, prefab_name, display_name, custom_name, build_state {current, last, complete, next}, condition, kind, type, damage_state, damage_state_class, max_damage, total_damage, damage_ratio, health_percent, band, damage {brute, burn, oxygen, hydration, starvation, toxic, radiation, decay, stun}, is_broken, broken_build_state, being_destroyed, pipe_burst, damage_record, position {x, y, z}, distance_m, networks [{kind, id}] | (BatchResult) results [{index, ok, error}], count, success_count, error_count | (HealthNetwork) network_id, kind, pieces, damaged_only, warning, broken_neighbour_count, broken_neighbours [{reference_id, prefab_name, position, pipe_burst, network, touches}], things [{reference_id, prefab_name, display_name, custom_name, build_state, condition, kind, type, damage_state, damage_state_class, max_damage, total_damage, damage_ratio, health_percent, band, damage, is_broken, broken_build_state, being_destroyed, pipe_burst, damage_record, position, distance_m, networks}], count, total, offset, limit, has_more
Example: inspect {what: "health", network: "5012345", kind: "pipe", damaged_only: true}
