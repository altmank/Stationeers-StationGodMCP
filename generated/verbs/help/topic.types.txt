Shared reply types. The small ones (Thing, Vec3, Error, Color, LogicType) are written out on the Types line of each stub that uses them. Job views come one per call: help {topic: "types", subtopic: "<name>"} or sg.help('types', '<name>'). Names: BuildJob, GasCheck, JobBusy, JobQueued, JobDropped, RefundPlan, RunJob, SwapJob, UpgradeJob, UndoJob.
