The 2 m cells of a box or room: what occupies each, the pieces, devices, ports and networks.
Options:
- box (list): Two corners; at most 20000 2 m cells.
- room (string): A room id.
- sections (list of cells | pieces | devices | networks | network_visibility | doors, default all): Only these parts.
- networks (boolean, default true): Include the networks of the pieces.
- include (list of refund | piece_cells): refund: each piece's removal refund; piece_cells: every small cell of a long piece.
- network (list): Only pieces on these networks.
- kind (list of cable | pipe | chute): Only pieces of these kinds.
- prefab (string or integer or array): Prefab: a whole name, a hash, or parts with * ('*Battery*', '*Tank*Insulated*': each part, any order); or a list of them.
- cell_detail (occupancy | full, default occupancy): occupancy: cells {at, empty, frame_id, faces, small}; full: cells {at, room_id, frame, walls, small, support} in place of those.
- occupied_only (boolean, default false): Only cells holding something.
- limit (integer, default 8): 2 m cells per page; with sections that leave cells out, every cell is read.
- offset (integer, default 0): Entries to skip.
A big area is many pages (1000 cells at most each), and output_file writes one page. For a whole area, page with offset (has_more) or use a smaller box per call.
Reply: legend, cells [{at, room_id, frame, walls, small, support, empty, frame_id, faces, small}], count, offset, limit, total, has_more, pieces [{reference_id, kind, prefab_name, at, cell_count, cell_box, cells, ends, network_id, grade, flow, carries, refund}], devices [{reference_id, prefab_name, display_name, at, rotation, ports, umbilical}], networks, network_visibility [{network_id, kind, pieces, cells, air_at, refund}], doors [{reference_id, prefab_name, display_name, faces, plane, band_m, port_cells}]
Example: survey {what: "area", room: "17", sections: ["pieces", "devices"]}
