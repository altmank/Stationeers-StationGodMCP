The 2 m cells of a box or room: what occupies each, the pieces, devices, ports and networks.
Options:
- box (list): Two corners; at most 20000 2 m cells.
- room (string): A room id.
- sections (list of cells | pieces | devices | networks | network_visibility | doors, default all): Only these parts.
- networks (boolean, default true): Include the networks of the pieces.
- include (list of refund | piece_cells | wall_planes | legend): refund: each piece's removal refund; piece_cells: every small cell of a long piece; wall_planes: small marks its '.' cells on the cell's -x, -y and -z face planes with that face's 'w', 'g' or 'x' (door over window over wall); legend: what each cell's keys and characters mean (also in help survey area).
- network (list): Only pieces on these networks.
- kind (list of cable | pipe | chute): Only pieces of these kinds.
- prefab (string or integer or array): Prefab: a whole name, a hash, or parts with * ('*Battery*', '*Tank*Insulated*': each part, any order); or a list of them.
- cell_detail (occupancy | full, default occupancy): occupancy: cells {at, empty, frame_id, faces, small}; full: cells {at, room_id, frame, walls, small, support} in place of those.
- occupied_only (boolean, default false): Only cells holding something.
- limit (integer, default 8): 2 m cells per page; with sections that leave cells out, every cell is read.
- offset (integer, default 0): Entries to skip.
A big area is many pages (1000 cells at most each), and output_file writes one page. For a whole area, page with offset (has_more) or use a smaller box per call.
Legend, cell_detail occupancy (include legend sends it in a reply): Each cell is a 2 m cell at its centre (odd metres). empty: true when it holds nothing. frame_id: its frame. faces: its faces +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z, each '.' nothing, 'w' wall, 'g' window, 'x' door (door over window over wall); left out when all are '.'. small: its 64 small cells (0.5 m) at -1, -0.5, 0 and +0.5 m from the centre along each axis, character index x + 4y + 16z: '.' empty, 'c' cable, 'p' pipe, 'b' cable and pipe, 'h' chute, 'd' device, 'o' another small-grid thing, 'r' a rocket's empty cell; left out when all are '.'. cell_detail full adds room, frame build state, face structure ids and the support string.
Legend, cell_detail full: Each cell is a 2 m cell at its centre (odd metres). small: its 64 small-grid cells (0.5 m) at -1, -0.5, 0 and +0.5 m from the centre along each axis (index 0 to 3; index 0 lies on the cell's minimum face plane, shared with the neighbour), character index x + 4y + 16z. '.' empty, 'c' cable, 'p' pipe, 'b' cable and pipe, 'h' chute, 'd' device, 'o' another small-grid thing (a mounted item, a rail), 'r' a rocket's empty cell (its fuselage decides which kind of piece it takes). Frames and walls never block cables or pipes; a device, chute or 'o' blocks both; a pipe blocks a cable (and a cable a pipe) only along the axis its ends lie on. A chute needs a cell with no cable, pipe, device, chute or 'o'. support: the same 64 cells by what holds a piece there up: 'i' inside a frame (every 2 m cell the small cell touches holds a frame: hidden in the frame's body), 'e' a frame edge or corner, 'f' on a frame's face (a frame's top face is the minimum plane of the cell above it), 'w' on a wall's plane, 'a' air (plan route with style supported avoids 'a' cells); over those, 'x' a door's keep-out (its face and the configured band either side inside its rectangle: the planners never route there without allow: ["door_keepout"]; the floor slab under a threshold is not in it) and 'g' a window's face (routes pay extra, crosses_window). A door's face is no wall support. network_visibility counts each listed network's cells the same way (inside, frame_surface, wall, air) and lists the floating (air) ones.
Reply: legend, cells [{at, room_id, frame, walls, small, support, empty, frame_id, faces, small}], count, offset, limit, total, has_more, pieces [{reference_id, kind, prefab_name, at, cell_count, cell_box, cells, ends, network_id, grade, flow, carries, refund}], devices [{reference_id, prefab_name, display_name, at, rotation, ports, umbilical}], networks, network_visibility [{network_id, kind, pieces, cells, air_at, refund}], doors [{reference_id, prefab_name, display_name, faces, plane, band_m, port_cells}]
Example: survey {what: "area", room: "17", sections: ["pieces", "devices"]}
