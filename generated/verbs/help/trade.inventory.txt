What each contact buys and sells: prices, stock, conditions, and what you hold that meets them.
Options:
- id (string or array): One contact; left out, every contact.
- card (string): The card whose holder's goods count; default the player's.
Any what that takes an id takes a list: one row per id, {results: [{index, ok, reference_id, payload | error}], count, success_count, error_count}, payload the one-id reply; a bad id is a row with error.
Reply: (Inventory) contacts [{reference_id, trader, display_name, shuttle, pad_size_tiles, contacted, buys, sells}] | (BatchResult) results [{index, ok, reference_id, contacts, ok, reference_id, error}], count, success_count, error_count
Example: trade {what: "inventory", id: "7"}
