Work out how, changing nothing: routes, removal prices, free spots, rocket flights, mining yields, aim angles. Read only.

whats:
- route: a cable, pipe or chute route between ends, ports or networks, reported as build would report it; answers build_arguments
- removal: price a removal of cable, pipe or chute pieces without doing it, ever: ids, cells or a whole network
- spot: free places to put one prefab you are about to build, near a point, on a face plane or a room's walls, checked by the game's cursor; answers build_arguments
- flight: fly a copy of a rocket to a node or pad, tick by tick: launch, hops, re-entry, landing, fuel, power and the verdict; the landing check
- mining: what a rocket can collect per site, by the game's formulas: yield per cycle and hour, what stops it
- aim: horizontal and Vertical that point a solar panel at the sun, or a satellite dish at a trader contact
