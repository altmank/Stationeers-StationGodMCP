Build new things as a player would, paying materials. Reports first; confirm: true builds and answers when the job is done.
A build that pays from the player's inventory warns materials_from_player.
A report carries report_only: true and lists every target with its current and new value.

whats:
- structure: any structure a kit builds (light, device, frame, wall, tank, rocket part) by prefab, at a point with a turn; several placements are one job
- cable: a cable run or pieces, as a coil builds it, of a grade; guards for bridging, splitting and overload
- pipe: a pipe run or pieces from the grade's kit, with the pipe guards and a gas check
- chute: a chute run or pieces from Kit (Chute), source to sink, with flow guards
- blueprint: paste a BlueprintMod blueprint at an anchor and turn, skipping placement checks (cheat)
- finish: build a placed structure on to a later build state, paying each state's materials

Every what here takes: confirm.
On every call: fields, omit, list_limits, output_file, whole, repeat, as_player (help {topic: "shaping"}).
help {verb: "build", what} gives a what's options, reply keys and an example.
