Any structure a kit builds (light, device, frame, wall, tank, rocket part) by prefab, at a point with a turn; several placements are one job.
Reports first; confirm: true does it.
Cheat when free: true.
Options:
- placements (list): Up to 64 {prefab, at, rotation, facing, up, face, orient, above_floor, build_state, label, color, logic}, built in order in one job; or one at the top level.
- prefab (string or integer): Prefab name or hash.
- at (array or object or string): A point, "looking_at", or a relative or look-face form (help positions).
- rotation (list): [x, y, z] degrees, multiples of 90.
- spread (object or array): {from, to, step} or a list: this prefab every step metres along each line; reports first.
- facing (+x | -x | +y | -y | +z | -z | toward_player | away_from_player | out_of_face | into_room): Where the front points.
- up (+x | -x | +y | -y | +z | -z, default +y (+z when facing is vertical)): Where the top points.
- face (+x | -x | +y | -y | +z | -z): Face-placed pieces (walls): the cell face it sits on.
- orient (object): {mount, upright, controls_toward, ports: [{role, index, type, toward}], flow: {from, to}}: choose the turn by intent. mount: wall, floor, ceiling or an axis; a target (controls_toward, toward, from, to): an axis, room, player, a point or a thing's id.
- above_floor (number): Bottom this many metres above the floor below at.
- build_state (string or integer): finished (default), first, or a state index.
- label (string): A name, as the Labeller writes it.
- color (string or integer): A colour name or index.
- logic (object): Logic values set once it is built, {On: 1, Setting: 50}, as write logic sets them; needs the finished build state.
- materials (string or array): Holder(s) that pay materials and take source refunds, tried in order; ids or "player". Default the player.
- free (boolean, default false): No materials. Cheat.
- allow (list of door_keepout | lint | pad): door_keepout: a keep-out cell is a warning; lint: lint problems are warnings the report marks PASSED; pad: a placement in a landing pad's approach is a problem; allowed, a PASSED warning.
- include (list of notes | footprint_cells): footprint_cells: each footprint's cells.
- verbose (boolean, default false): Report: layouts a refused report leaves out; real run: the whole preflight.
- acknowledge_gas_lost (string): The job id whose gas loss you accept (with the user's agreement); lifts the gas hold.
- from_plan (string): A ref of a plan route or plan spot reply ("r9"; one spot: "r9.spots.2", default the first): build its build_arguments; options given here replace the plan's, and a run given (waypoints, cells, piece, pieces) replaces its run.
- check_only (boolean, default false): Report only, never builds, even confirmed or in a confirmed run.
- confirm (boolean, default false): true acts; false only reports, whatever the call names (typed ids included). Left out, the write rule decides: typed ids act at once where the verb acts on them; a ref, network or spread reports.
- queue (boolean, default true): Wait in line behind a running job (up to 8) instead of answering busy.
- max_wait (number, default 30): Seconds to wait for a confirmed job to finish before answering; 0 answers with its job id at once.
A build that pays from the player's inventory (materials left out, or "player") carries the warning materials_from_player. With from_plan, what may be left out: it comes from the plan.
The report gives each placement's position as the game's cursor snaps it (the row's cell and placements[].position). A placement checks against the run's earlier placements as standing: a clash with one (overlaps_placement), the frame it needs (supported_by_placement), and with above_floor, a frame or floor plate an earlier placement puts down as its floor.
placed: on a job reply the new ids (a run's log.placed, a structure job's result.placed; a brief poll counts them in log.placed_count), on a run report a count of the cells it would build.
Reply: (JobQueued) a JobQueued (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "JobQueued"}) | (BuildJob) a BuildJob (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "BuildJob"}) | (PlaceReport) ready, problems [{code, message, index, reference_id}], warnings [{code, message, index, reference_id}], placements [{index, prefab_name, prefab_hash, display_name, placement, position, orientation, face, ports, build_state, build_states, label, color, cost, layout, orient, resolved}], materials [{prefab_name, needed, available, paid_by}], from {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, free, notes | (RunJob) a RunJob (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "RunJob"}) | (JobBusy) a JobBusy (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "JobBusy"}) | (JobDropped) a JobDropped (help {topic: "types", subtopic: "JobDropped"})
Example: build {what: "structure", prefab: "StructureWallLight", at: [716, 203, 668], facing: "-x", confirm: true}
