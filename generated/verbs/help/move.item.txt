Items and kits (a kit such as Kit (Battery Large) is an item; a built structure is not) into a slot or the player's hands with the game's own moves; into or out of a vault or silo store.
Cheat when force: true.
Options:
- id (string or array): The item(s); for a vault or silo deposit, whole things by id.
- quantity (number): Slot move: how many off the stack, default the whole item. Out of a vault: grams of ingot or a count of ore or ice; out of a silo: an item count; required for both.
- to (string): The holder that gets them: a container, belt, suit, "player", a vault or a silo.
- slot (integer or string, default auto): A slot index of to, or "auto" (the default). Given, the move is a slot move even into a vault's or silo's own slots; force needs an index.
- merge (boolean, default true): Join a matching stack in the slot.
- force (boolean, default false): Into an empty slot by index even when hidden. Cheat.
- allow (list of in_use | ground): in_use: take a part its device is using; ground: what does not fit goes on the ground.
- moves (list): Up to 64 moves in order, each {id, to, slot, quantity, merge, allow}; slot auto when left out.
- source (string): A vault or silo to take from.
- prefab (string or integer or array): Into a store: filter; out of a store: the item to take (whole name or hash).
- reagent (string): Out of a vault: the stored reagent (Iron, Steel).
- items (array or string): Store moves: lines {id, quantity} (deposit) or {prefab, reagent, quantity} (vault to vault: grams of ingot, a count of ore or ice; "all" moves every line; required vault to vault, where a top-level prefab and quantity become one line).
- name (string): Part of the shown name or label, any case.
- location (any | ground | player | stored, default any): Into a store: filter.
- within (string): Into a store: only things inside this holder.
- near (string or array): "player" only.
- radius (number): Metres around near.
- category (any | ingot | ore | ice, default any): Into a vault: filter.
- at_most (integer, default vault 256, silo 64): Into a store: move at most this many things, nearest first.
- confirm (boolean, default false): Typed ids act at once; with a filter or a ref, true acts and left out reports.
Which handler runs: with slot or moves, a slot move. Otherwise a vault or silo as to or source makes a store move; everything else is a slot move. The reply's first key route names it: slot, into_vault, out_of_vault, vault_to_vault, into_silo or out_of_silo.
Filters (prefab parts, name, location, within, near, category) work for moves into a store; slot moves take ids.
Out of a vault: move {what: "item", source: "4191359", prefab: "ItemIronIngot", quantity: 500, to: "player"}.
Into a silo by filter: move {what: "item", to: "5200100", prefab: "*Ore*", location: "player"}.
Reply: (ItemMoved) route, index, ok, reference_id, display_name, from {id, slot}, to {id, slot}, quantity_moved, merged_into, destination_reference_id, warning | (BatchResult) route, results [{index, ok, error}], count, success_count, error_count | (VaultDeposit) route, vault {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, via {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, matched, skipped, truncated, items {results, count, success_count, error_count}, stock [{kind, prefab_name, display_name, reagent, before, change, after}] | (VaultWithdraw) route, vault {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, via {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, to {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, quantity, placed [{where, slot, quantity, reference_id}], stock [{kind, prefab_name, display_name, reagent, before, change, after}] | (VaultTransfer) route, from_vault {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, from_via {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, to_vault {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, to_via {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, items {results, count, success_count, error_count}, partial_count | (SiloDeposit) route, silo {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, on_off, powered, matched, skipped, truncated, items {results, count, success_count, error_count}, count {before, after} | (SiloWithdraw) route, silo {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, on_off, powered, to {reference_id, prefab_name, display_name}, quantity, taken [{index, quantity, left, whole, children}], placed [{where, slot, quantity, reference_id}], stock {prefab_name, display_name, before, change, after}, count {before, after}
Example: move {what: "item", id: "3311223", to: "player"}
