StationGod's help, from the MCP server: help {} lists verbs and topics; {verb} a verb's whats and options; {verb, what} one what in full; {topic} a shared topic.
Options:
- verb (string): A verb: its whats and shared options.
- lang (python): python: the stub for sg calls in a script; leave it out for a direct call.
- what (string): With verb: that what's options, reply keys and an example.
- topic (string): A topic (targets, paging, writes, shaping, refs, errors, positions, turns, refunds, jobs, networks...).
- subtopic (string): A subtopic of topic; types takes one type name (BuildJob).
Reply: verb, what, lang, topic, subtopic, text
Example: help {verb: "find"}
