Swap things already built, in place: walls or frames, upgrade or tidy cable, pipe and chute networks. Reports first; confirm: true does it.
A report carries report_only: true and lists every target with its current and new value.

whats:
- wall: replace walls and windows with another wall or window prefab without opening a face
- frame: replace frames with another frame prefab, or finish unfinished frames, without opening a cell
- cable: upgrade cable in place (into heavy or super_heavy) or tidy a network (tidy: remove dead ends, loops...)
- pipe: upgrade pipe in place (into insulated, or repair burst pieces) or tidy a network, keeping its contents
- chute: remove chute pieces no item can pass to a consumer; junctions left with one way become straights

Every what here takes: confirm, id, include, materials, max_wait, queue, refund_to, rows, skip_unmatched, verbose.
Shaping on every call: fields, omit, list_limits, output_file, whole, repeat (help {topic: "shaping"}).
help {verb: "swap", what} gives a what's options, reply keys and an example.
